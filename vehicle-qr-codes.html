QR Codes for Cars and Vehicles in Nigeria

Explore vehicle QR code stickers for cars, taxis, motorcycles and keke: create a vehicle profile, scan a code and connect privately.

What is a vehicle QR code?

A unique QR sticker that opens a vehicle’s Roadily profile in a browser. It does not publicly reveal the owner’s phone number.

Where should the sticker go?

Place a small, clear sticker at a safe, visible location on the vehicle, such as near the rear badge, without obstructing lights, glass or the licence plate.

Can I scan it from another vehicle?

Only scan while stationary or let a passenger scan. Do not use a phone while driving.

Does the QR code work outside my computer?

Yes, once Roadily has a public HTTPS domain and the printed code links to that domain. Localhost URLs only work on the same device.

What kinds of vehicles are supported?

The concept supports cars, taxis, minibuses, motorcycles and keke. Vehicle ownership and authorisation checks remain necessary before enabling money features.
