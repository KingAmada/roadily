Contact a Driver Privately Through a Vehicle QR Code

Connect to registered vehicles without publicly sharing a phone number. Learn how Roadily opt-in networking and request approval work.

Can I message a car owner by plate number?

Only if the registered vehicle profile has opted into networking and the user accepts the request. A plate number alone does not reveal a private phone number.

Can I contact a taxi or keke driver?

Vehicle-associated messaging can support professional enquiries, networking and private connections where the person using that profile has opted in.

Is my identity shared automatically?

No. Roadily vehicle profiles do not automatically expose phone numbers, and users can choose whether to reveal their display names.

What if I receive unwanted requests?

Profiles can disable networking; requests need acceptance. Users should not scan codes while driving.
