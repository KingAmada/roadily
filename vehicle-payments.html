Vehicle QR Payments for Taxis, Keke & Drivers

Roadily is developing QR-linked vehicle payment experiences for taxis, keke, motorcycles and everyday drivers. Read the product roadmap.

What are vehicle payments?

A vehicle-associated payment identity could let passengers pay taxi or keke fares through a QR profile rather than exchanging account details.

Are fare transfers active today?

No. The app does not process live payments, keep a verified balance or transfer withdrawals until its payment-provider API is connected.

Will the public see the owner’s banking details?

The intended design separates public vehicle identities from private and verified bank beneficiary details.

What protects vehicle owners?

Before enabling money flows, Roadily needs ownership or driver-authorisation verification, payment-provider compliance and robust transaction records.
