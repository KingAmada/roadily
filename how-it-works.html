How Roadily Works

Learn how Roadily vehicle QR profiles work, how to find vehicles by number plate, request private connections and discover planned driver rewards.

Create a vehicle identity

Register a vehicle and receive a unique Roadily QR profile. The QR code is a web link to the vehicle profile, not a public phone number.

Scan or search a vehicle

Use a phone camera to scan a Roadily sticker or search a vehicle plate in Roadily. Only registered vehicles can be found.

Choose what others can see

The profile holder can choose whether to display their name, enable networking or show selected public features.

Connect after approval

A vehicle user who enables networking can accept or decline requests; chats require acceptance.

Reward and payments roadmap

Roadily plans driver tips and fare collection. Live payments and withdrawals are disabled until approved payment infrastructure is integrated.
