What can you do with Roadily?

Roadily is a digital vehicle identity and private connection platform. A Roadily QR sticker links to a registered vehicle profile. Drivers can manage what others see and choose whether to accept connection requests.

Scan a vehicle QR code

Scan a Roadily QR sticker to visit a vehicle profile, or search by its registered plate number. Camera scanning works on supported browsers with permission.

Common questions about Roadily

How do I scan a QR code on a car?

Open Roadily's scanner on a supported phone, grant camera permission and point it at a Roadily QR sticker while safely stationary. You can also enter a registered plate number in vehicle search.

Can I contact a driver without their phone number?

Yes, if the vehicle's user opts into networking, you can send a request through the vehicle profile. Chat opens only after that request is accepted.

Can drivers receive tips or taxi payments?

Driver rewards and vehicle payments are planned Roadily features, but actual transfers are currently disabled until a regulated payment integration is completed.

How is a vehicle's ownership verified?

Roadily's current vehicle registration is self-declared. Additional ownership or driver-authorisation verification is required before financial functionality is enabled.
